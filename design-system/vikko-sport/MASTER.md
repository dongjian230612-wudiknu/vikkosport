# Vikko Sport Design System (Light Retail Override)

> Generated by UI UX Pro Max, then overridden to match sports-eyewear retail norms
> ([SportRx](https://www.sportrx.com/), [Oakley](https://www.oakley.com/), [Rudy Project](https://www.rudyprojectna.com/), [100%](https://www.100percent.com/)).

## Direction
- **Mode:** Light retail (white / light gray canvas). Avoid full-page OLED black.
- **Pattern:** Full-bleed hero → Shop by Category → Shop by Sport → Product grid → Rx band → Trust.
- **Nav:** Sunglasses + Eyeglasses mega-menus only.
- **Typography:** Rubik (display) + Nunito Sans (body).
- **Colors:** Black CTAs, white surfaces, accent `#0284c7` sparingly (RX / brand mark).
- **Imagery:** Product and sport photography as primary visual anchors.

## Anti-patterns
- Entire page black backgrounds
- Purple AI gradients / cream-serif retail clichés
- Stats / promo clutter in the first viewport
